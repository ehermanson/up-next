## Description (4000)

Up Next keeps track of the movies and TV shows you want to watch, the ones you're in the middle of, and the ones you've finished.

Add a title and Up Next fills in the details: poster, cast, ratings, where it's streaming, and when the next season airs.

YOUR WATCHLIST
• Separate tabs for TV Shows and Movies, in the order you want to watch them.
• Shows you're partway through sit in Watching, at the top.
• Mark TV seasons watched one at a time. When you finish every aired season, the show moves to Watched, and it comes back on its own when a new season starts.
• Returning Soon and Coming Soon show premieres and releases from your watchlist over the next two weeks.
• Rate what you’ve watched: thumbs up, meh, or thumbs down.

SHARE WITH ONE OTHER PERSON
• Share your whole watchlist with one other Apple Account over iCloud. You both add, check off, and reorder the same titles.
• Get a notification when the other person adds or finishes something, and see recent changes in Activity.
• Your streaming services are shared too, so "on my services" means the same thing to both of you.

WHERE TO WATCH
• Pick the streaming services you have. Each title shows where it's streaming, with your services listed first.
• Filter your watchlist or Discover to what you can watch right now.
• Choose your region for accurate availability.

TITLE DETAILS
• Cast, with a page for each actor's other movies and shows.
• Every episode in every season, plus ratings for each season and episode.
• More Like This, trailers, and links to TMDB and IMDb.

SEARCH THE WAY YOU THINK
• Type a title, or describe what you want: "hulu hockey comedy", "90s heist movies", "korean dramas", "tom hanks christmas".
• Ask for more of something you liked: "shows like Ted Lasso", "movies like Inception on Netflix".
• Streaming services, genres, decades, countries and people are all understood, and results show what's on your services.
• On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Up Next also reads plot descriptions ("movie where a guy relives the same day") and ranks "shows like…" by feel, not just subject. It all happens on your device; nothing you type leaves it.

DISCOVER
• Trending, Airing This Week, In Theaters, Top Rated, New Releases, and Recommended For You based on your watchlist.
• Add any title in one tap.

COLLECTIONS
• Group titles however you like: Christmas movies, Studio Ghibli, date night.
• Suggestions for what else fits each collection.
• Each collection tracks what you've watched in it separately from your Up Next queue.

ALSO
• Built for iOS 26, with full layouts on iPad.
• Dark, light, or system appearance.
• Syncs across your devices with iCloud.
• No account, no ads, no tracking.

Data and images provided by The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## What's New in This Version (4000)

Search now understands descriptions, not just titles.

• Describe what you want to watch: "slasher movies", "scary shows on Netflix", "90s heist films", "british crime shows", "kids shows on Disney+".
• Ask for more like something: "shows like Ted Lasso", "something similar to The Bear", "movies like Inception on Netflix".
• Search by people: "tom hanks comedies", "christopher nolan", "zendaya".
• Streaming services, genres, decades, countries and languages are all understood, in any order and in plain words.
• A title you name still comes first, even with a service beside it ("the bear hulu").
• On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Up Next also reads plot descriptions ("show about a chemistry teacher who makes meth") and ranks "shows like…" results by tone and feel. Everything runs on your device.

Also in this version:
• Search results put well-known titles first and stop obscure same-name titles from crowding them out.
• Small fixes throughout.

## App Review notes

No sign-in is required. Movie and TV data comes from The Movie Database (TMDB) API.

Search by description works on every device. On devices with Apple Intelligence it additionally uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework to read the query; no text is sent to any server other than TMDB's search API (the same requests a title search makes).

Sharing uses CloudKit sharing and needs two devices signed in to different Apple Accounts. To test it: Settings → Sharing → Share Your Watchlist, send the invitation to the second account, and tap the link on the second device to join. Everything else works with a single account, or with no iCloud account (local only).
