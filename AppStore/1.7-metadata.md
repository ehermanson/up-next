## What's New in This Version (≤ 4000)

Up Next 1.7 is a big one — smarter recommendations, an iPad that finally feels like an iPad, and a lot of polish.

RECOMMENDATIONS THAT LEARN FROM YOU
• Recommended For You now weighs what you've rated: thumbs-up titles pull in more like them, thumbs-down titles push similar ones away.
• It learns your genres from your own list and leans toward the services you actually subscribe to.
• Obscure titles with a handful of votes no longer outrank the good stuff.
• The detail page's Similar and Recommended rows are now one clean "More Like This" row — no duplicates, and nothing you've already added.

BUILT FOR iPAD
• Full-width grids on iPad, a tab bar that collapses into a sidebar, and a roomy detail view. Resizes cleanly in Stage Manager.

CAUGHT UP MEANS CAUGHT UP
• When a show announces a new season that hasn't aired yet, it stays in Watched with a "Season 4 announced" note (or the premiere date once it's set) instead of jumping back into Up Next. It returns to Up Next by itself when the season starts.

MORE
• Choose your region for streaming availability, right next to your services.
• Pull to refresh on Discover always fetches fresh results.
• Streaming logos keep a consistent order instead of shuffling.
• Faster, steadier search — the list no longer jumps while you type.
• New app icon designed for Liquid Glass, with dark and tinted variants.

## Description (≤ 4000)

Up Next is the watchlist you'd keep in Notes — if Notes knew what was on Netflix.

Add the movies and shows you mean to watch. Up Next fills in the rest: posters, ratings, where to stream, which season you're on, and when the next episode airs.

UP NEXT, NOT A BACKLOG
• One list per tab — TV Shows and Movies — in the order you want to watch them.
• Airing Soon and Coming Soon strips surface what's landing in the next 30 days.
• Mark seasons as you go. Partially watched shows stay in Up Next; caught-up shows move to Watched and come back when a new season starts.
• Rate what you've watched with a thumbs up, meh, or thumbs down, and jot a note.

KNOWS YOUR SERVICES
• Pick the streaming services you subscribe to. Every title shows where you can watch it on *your* services, and one tap filters your list to what you can watch right now.
• Choose your region for accurate availability anywhere in the world.

DISCOVER
• Trending, Airing This Week, In Theaters, Top Rated and New Releases — filtered to your services if you want.
• Recommended For You learns from your ratings and your genres, and leans toward what's on your services.
• Browse by genre, sort by popularity, rating or release date.

COLLECTIONS
• Themed collections — Christmas, Halloween, Shows for the Kids — kept separate from your Up Next queue, each with its own watched state.

MADE FOR iPHONE AND iPAD
• Designed for iOS 26 with Liquid Glass. Full-width grids and a sidebar on iPad.
• iCloud sync across your devices.
• No account. No ads. No tracking. Ever.
